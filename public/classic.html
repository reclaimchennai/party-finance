Holding Political Parties Financially Accountable.

Official public financial data for Tamil Nadu regional parties (DMK, AIADMK, TVK, NTK, PMK, DMDK, MDMK, VCK, MNM, IUML) and national parties operating in the state (BJP, INC, CPI(M), CPI, AAP, BSP). Every figure is verified from official signed statutory filings with the Election Commission of India, CEO Tamil Nadu RUPP portal, and Supreme Court mandated SBI disclosure sheets.

Data Integrity & Statutory Basis: All data presented on this dashboard is compiled directly from official public submissions to the Election Commission of India (ECI) under Section 29C of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 (Form 24A Contribution Reports), Section 139(4B) of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (Annual Audit Statements & Balance Sheets), CEO Tamil Nadu Registered Unrecognized Political Parties (RUPPs) filings, and the Supreme Court of India judgement in Association for Democratic Reforms & CPI(M) v. Union of India (Writ Petition (Civil) No. 880/2017) mandating SBI disclosure of Electoral Bonds.

Direct sources: ECI Contribution Reports · ECI Annual Audit Reports · CEO Tamil Nadu RUPPs Portal · ECI Electoral Bonds Disclosure · Old ECI Archive (2003–2022).
